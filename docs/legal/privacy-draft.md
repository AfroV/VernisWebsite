## 2. What we collect and why

| What | When | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone, shipping address, plug type | You buy a frame (Stripe checkout) | To deliver your order and contact you about it | Contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Payment details | You pay | Processed by Stripe. We never see your full card number | Contract, Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Order records | After purchase | Bookkeeping law requires us to keep them | Legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c) |
| Email address | You join the waitlist | To email you when a wave opens and before it closes | Consent, Art. 6(1)(a) |
| Anonymous visit statistics | You visit the site | To see how many people visit and buy | Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) |
| Emails you send us | You write to us | To answer you | Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) |

## 3. Your own art stays on your device

When you try your own image, GIF, SVG or video on the website, the file is opened in your
browser only. It is **not uploaded** to us or anyone else, and it is gone when you close the
page.

## 4. Who we share data with

- **Stripe** (payments and checkout) — stripe.com/privacy
- **Mailchimp / Intuit** (waitlist emails) — only if you sign up
- **Plausible Analytics** (visit statistics, hosted in the EU; no cookies and no personal data)
- **[Shipping carrier — fill in]** (name, address, phone, to deliver the parcel)
- **Our accountant and the tax authorities**, as bookkeeping law requires

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

## 5. Transfers outside the EEA

Stripe and Mailchimp may process data in the USA. They are certified under the EU–US Data
Privacy Framework and use the EU standard contractual clauses. **[CONFIRM — QUESTION 1]**

## 6. How long we keep it

- Order and payment records: 5 years after the end of the financial year (bokføringsloven).
- Waitlist: until you unsubscribe (every email has a link), then removed.
- Emails with us: as long as needed to help you, then at most 1 year.
- Visit statistics: aggregated and anonymous; kept by Plausible.

## 7. Cookies

The site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Plausible counts visits without cookies.
Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page, which is covered by Stripe's policy.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent
for the waitlist at any time. Email av@vernis.art and we reply within 30 days. You can
also complain to Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.no).

## 9. Crypto wallets (optional)

If you connect a wallet to your frame, the frame reads public blockchain data on the device
itself. We don't receive it. We never ask for private keys or seed phrases.
