## Questions for the reviewer (answer these first)

1. **Norwegian VAT (MVA).** Sales to customers in Norway are domestic and carry 25% MVA, and
   Norwegian consumers must be shown prices *including* MVA. Exports outside Norway carry no
   Norwegian MVA; the buyer pays import VAT on arrival. Options:
   - (a) one price for everyone (€600 / €750), MVA included for Norwegian buyers (lower margin on
     Norwegian orders);
   - (b) separate Norway checkout links with MVA added on top (€750 / €937.50).
   Which do we use? The draft below is written for **(a)**.
2. **Warranty period.** Forbrukerkjøpsloven gives 2 years, or 5 years for goods meant to last
   "vesentlig lenger". Is a €600–750 display frame a 5-year item? The draft says 5 years for the
   brass case and 2 years for electronics, with 5 years where the law requires it.
3. **Kit vs assembled.** We sell a kit of individually CE-marked components plus a brass case;
   the buyer assembles it. Confirm this wording is enough, and that the brass case needs no
   marking of its own.
4. **Pre-order wave.** We take payment during the sales window and order parts afterwards.
   Is it acceptable to charge at checkout, given a stated delivery time? (Draft: yes, with a
   promised latest delivery date and the right to cancel with full refund if we miss it.)
5. **Delivery time.** What do we promise? **[X] weeks after the window closes.**
6. **EU customers.** Our terms follow Norwegian law, but EU consumers keep the protections of
   their own country's mandatory law. Is the sentence in section 12 enough?
7. **Return shipping and the 14-day right of withdrawal** — the buyer pays return shipping
