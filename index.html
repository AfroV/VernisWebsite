Why it stays yours

No account, no subscription

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to renew. The frame works on its own.

The files live on the frame

Most digital art is shown from someone else's server. Vernis keeps a copy of every work on its own storage, pinned with IPFS for collectors who want to know how.

Open source, if we're gone

The software and enclosure files are public. If Vernis disappeared tomorrow, your frame would keep running, and anyone could maintain it.

Rather build one yourself?

The enclosure files and software are open source. Bring your own Raspberry Pi and display, and build a frame from scratch. No purchase needed.

Questions

What do I get in the box?

The handmade metal frame, a 4-inch touch screen, a Raspberry Pi 5, 128 GB of storage with the software pre-installed, a power supply with your plug type, and an assembly guide. Putting it together takes a few minutes and needs no soldering. Your signed, numbered certificate is delivered digitally.

Which one should I choose?

Vernis Side is the original size, with ventilation and the cable leaving from the side. Vernis Rear is a little larger and routes the cable out the back, so it looks clean from every angle you'd normally see it.

Why a sales window?

We build in waves. Collecting orders first lets us buy parts at a fair price and make exactly as many frames as people want, with nothing left over.

Do I need crypto or a wallet?

No. You pay in euros by card. If you collect NFTs, the frame can show the works you own and back them up locally, but it's not required.

What can it show?

Still images, GIFs and video loops. Square works fill the screen; other shapes can be cropped or shown whole.

What happens if Vernis the company disappears?

Nothing changes for your frame. The files are stored on it, the software is open source and it runs on standard hardware you can maintain yourself.

How long does the storage last?

Like any SD card, the storage wears out over time. The software is free to download, so you can flash a new card and re-sync your collection at any time.
