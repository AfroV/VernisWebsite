3. Your own art stays on your device

When you try your own image or video on the website, the file opens in your browser only. It is not uploaded to us or anyone else, and it is gone when you close the page.

4. Who we share data with

  • Stripe, for checkout and payment. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • Bring, and DHL if you choose express shipping: your name, address, phone and email, to deliver the parcel and send tracking.
  • Plausible Analytics, hosted in the EU: visit counts with no cookies and no personal data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and Google Fonts serves its fonts. Like any web server, they receive your IP address when your browser loads the page.
  • Our accountant and the tax authorities, as bookkeeping law requires.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

Stripe, GitHub and Google may process data in the USA. They are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and use the EU standard contractual clauses. For parcels to countries outside the EEA, your shipping details go to the carriers in that country so they can deliver it.

6. Wallet addresses and the blockchain

A wallet address is public by nature. If we send you a Collector Pass, the transfer is recorded on the Ethereum blockchain, where it is public and can't be changed or deleted by anyone, including us. Leave the wallet field empty if you don't want that. We never ask for private keys or seed phrases.

7. How long we keep it

  • Order and payment records: 5 years after the end of the financial year, as bookkeeping law requires.
  • The list for the next wave: until you ask to be removed.
  • Emails with us: as long as needed to help you, then at most 1 year.
  • Visit counts: aggregated and anonymous.

8. Cookies

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies, and Plausible counts visits without cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page, covered by Stripe's policy.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent to the list at any time. Email admin@25time.no and we'll reply within 30 days. Some order records must be kept for bookkeeping even if you ask us to delete them.

You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

10. Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new date.
